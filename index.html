Welcome to PeaMC!

PeaMC is a small, semi-vanilla Minecraft survival server, offering Java and Bedrock cross-platform compatibility on the latest version of Minecraft. We invite you to stay and embark on adventures within this world alongside us.

Rules:

No Exploiting/Hacking:
No, using cheats to gain an unfair advantage
Be Respectful (Hate/Discrimination):
No racism, homophobia, spamming, slurs,
threats, bullying or harassment of any kind.
No Griefing/Stealing:
Pvp is still allow, but blowing up somebody's base
and stealing items from their house isn't

Plugins:

  • Teleportation
    • /TPA, To get around easier.
  • Teams
    • /Parties, To promote teamwork!
  • Graves
    • To make it easier to find your items after death
  • Multiplayer Sleep
    • Allowing half of the server to sleep to skip night.
  • Bounties
    • /Bounties, To promote vengeance!
  • Proximity VoiceChat
    • using the Simple Voice Chat mod.
